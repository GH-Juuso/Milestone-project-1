Generated Rental Database Ingestion Package

Row counts:
- Employee.csv: 151 rows (150 employees + dummy employee_id 0)
- Customer.csv: 10,000 rows
- Equipment.csv: 80 rows
- Rental_station.csv: 200 rows
- Inventory.csv: 15,000 rows
- Rental_transaction.csv: 40,000 rows
- RentalLine.csv: 100,000 rows

Geographic coverage:
- North America: Canada, USA, Mexico
- Europe: France, UK, Spain, Germany, Netherlands, Italy, Portugal, Sweden, Norway
- Asia-Pacific: Japan, Singapore, India, Australia
- Middle East & Africa: UAE, South Africa

Data diversity:
- 20+ cities across 15+ countries
- Mix of staffed and unstaffed stations (~25% unstaffed)
- Rental durations ranging from same-day rentals to multi-day rentals
- Rental amounts ranging from low-cost safety equipment rentals to premium vehicle rentals
- Equipment categories include E-Bikes, E-Scooters, Safety Equipment, and Accessories
- Inventory conditions include Excellent, Good, Fair, and Maintenance Required
- Inventory statuses include Available, Rented, and Maintenance
- Employees have varied hiring dates and salaries
- Customers have unique contact information

Data model:
- Equipment represents product models/types
- Inventory represents physical rentable assets
- Transactions represent rental orders
- RentalLine represents rented items within a transaction

Referential integrity:
- Employee → Rental_station
- Equipment → Inventory
- Customer → Rental_transaction
- Rental_station → Rental_transaction (pickup & return stations)
- Rental_transaction → RentalLine
- Inventory → RentalLine

Notes:
- Equipment catalog intentionally limited to fewer than 100 records.
- Dummy employee (employee_id = 0) is assigned to all unstaffed stations.
- All foreign key relationships are valid and ingestion-ready.
- Dataset is designed for SQL analytics, reporting, dashboarding, ETL, and data warehousing exercises.
